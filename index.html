The instructor

Nearly two decades in the saddle, all of it local.

Thom grew up riding the bridleways around Leicester before moving into eventing and showjumping as a teenager. Now BHS-qualified and coaching full-time, he splits his week between nervous first-timers finding their seat and established riders sharpening their eye for a line of fences.

His height turns out to be useful for more than getting into the saddle — Thom can read a course of fences from further back than most, and it shows in how early his riders learn to see a stride.

What's on offer

Lessons built around the rider in front of him.

Private lessons

One-to-one flatwork and jumping, paced to you. Popular with beginners building confidence and with riders returning to the saddle after time away.

45 minutes · school or hack out

Small group sessions

Two to four riders of a similar level, working the same exercises together. A sociable way to progress, with plenty of individual correction.

60 minutes · matched by ability

Competition coaching

Course-walking, striding and ringcraft for riders heading into affiliated or unaffiliated showjumping and dressage. Arena hire and warm-up support on show days.

by arrangement · own horse required

Young rider foundations

Balance, position and horsemanship for younger riders, taught on steady school ponies before they move up to a horse of their own.

30–45 minutes · ages 6 and up
